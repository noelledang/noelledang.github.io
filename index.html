Selected case studies / How I solve problems

Behind the solution.

A closer look at how I investigate technical issues, connect the dots, and help customers move forward.

01

When Shopify orders stopped syncing

Investigating missing order confirmations between Shopify and a referral platform.

SQL Integrations Cross-team escalation
Read case study

Challenge. A client reported that Shopify orders were no longer appearing in the referral platform, affecting visibility into referral activity and rewards.

Investigation. I reviewed SQL reports and historical order activity to establish that orders had previously been received, then identified when incoming confirmations stopped. I coordinated with the relevant teams to narrow down where the data flow was failing.

Action. I documented the evidence for Engineering, communicated the investigation status to the client, and proposed manual referral credits as an interim workaround.

Outcome. The escalation included a clearer timeline and supporting data, while the client had a proposed path to address affected rewards during the investigation. I did not independently deploy an integration fix.

02

The mystery of the RDC queue

Tracing unexpected manual-review volume in a digital banking application.

Digital banking Pattern analysis Escalation
Read case study

Challenge. Approximately 98% of remote deposit capture submissions were entering manual review, despite no obvious error in the user interface.

Investigation. I tested the issue, investigated its intermittent behavior, and checked with the vendor. Comparing affected clients revealed that they were on the same application pod.

Action. I escalated the pod-level pattern and relevant settings for further investigation rather than treating each client's report as an isolated issue.

Outcome. The investigation narrowed the likely scope to a shared environment and gave the receiving team a more targeted starting point. Final root cause and remediation were outside my confirmed findings.

03

Finding discrepancies in HR data

Using self-taught SQL to investigate benefits, enrollment, and access issues.

SQL joins Data integrity HR technology
Read case study

Challenge. Customer-reported issues involved missing benefits, enrollment-file mismatches, and access discrepancies that could not be explained by the interface alone.

Investigation. I taught myself SQL joins and subqueries so I could compare records, review imported file values, and investigate discrepancies directly in the underlying data.

Action. I checked source-file formatting, account and enrollment records, and relevant access information before fixing permitted data issues or preparing evidence for escalation.

Outcome. This approach enabled more independent investigation and better-informed handoffs, reducing reliance on others for basic data checks.
